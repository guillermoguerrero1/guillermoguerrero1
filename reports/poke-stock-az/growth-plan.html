What the numbers say

  • The giveaway is 70% of all interactions the account has ever had (564 of 804), with 133 shares. Giveaways work. The problem is that nothing keeps the momentum going afterward.
  • Questions beat announcements. "Which event are you attending?" reached 1,512 people, 7× a typical single-show announcement (119–264). Posts that list several shows did well too (1,048).
  • Your reels aren't working yet. The 4 reels average 434 reach, about the same as photos. They're table shots and thank-yous with no hook in the first second. Reels with pulls, prices and people should beat them easily.
  • Nobody saves your posts. No post has more than 5 saves. Saves come from useful content: price lists, buy lists, show calendars people keep.
  • Posting is steady but thin. 13 posts from Mar 22 to Sep 20 is about 2 a month, almost all of them show announcements. Nothing has been posted since Sep 20.
Audit

What's working and what isn't

Keep doing

  • A full show calendar. In six months you've worked Pewter TCG, Power Summer Splash, The Power show, West Valley Card Rally, Collector's Grid, AZ Hobby Expo and three Cactus shows. That's a lot of in-person reach.
  • Giveaways with pickup at the booth. They brought 2,298 reach and 94 follows in one day, and they bring the winner to your table.
  • Asking your audience questions. It's your best post that isn't a giveaway.
  • Creator seeding. Packs sent to family rip creators on Facebook (Beforeskoolripz, a lunch-break rip series) get you mentioned by name.

Fix

  • Zero link taps. 447 visitors in 28 days, and none could tap through to inventory, a hold form or contact info. This is the cheapest fix.
  • Growth stops between shows. 4 follows in the 10 days after the Sep show.
  • Mostly announcements. No inventory, prices or "we buy" posts, so there's no reason to check back or save anything.
  • Reels without a hook. They open on a table and end on a thank-you.
  • Name collision. "Poke Stock" reads like the restock-alert pages (PokeStocked, Poke Stocks).

Cadence

  • 3 posts a week, up from about 2 a month. Shoot 20–30 clips per show weekend and you'll have enough.
  • Inventory carousel every Tuesday so followers learn when to look.
  • Stories most days: polls, "new in," countdown stickers before shows. Stories reach existing followers, who currently see only 294 of your 3,439 reach.
  • Cross-post Reels to TikTok and Facebook. Post your monthly show calendar in the Arizona Pokemon TCG Facebook group.

Giveaways

Giveaways: keep the format, fix the follow-through

  • Keep pickup at the show. It's why your followers are 93% local.
  • Run one a month, not just when you have a big product. Smaller prizes (a booster bundle plus booth credit) are fine. The Aug 31 giveaway's 133 shares did the reaching for you.
  • Swap "tag 3 friends" for "tag 1 friend who'd come to the show." That keeps the reach and gets more people who'll actually come.
  • The day after the draw, post something useful (inventory with prices or a show calendar) so new followers see a reason to stay.
  • Track: follows on giveaway day vs. follower count 14 days later (Insights → Followers).
