Audit

What's working and what isn't

Keep doing

  • Monthly booth at the Cactus TCG Show. Free entry, 115+ tables, and AZFamily coverage bring thousands of local collectors each weekend. Few online-only accounts can reach that audience.
  • Prize pickup at the booth. Every winner walks to the table, which brings in foot traffic and keeps the giveaway local.
  • Creator seeding. A few packs sent to family rip creators bring video, a shout-out, and new audiences for little cost.
  • The scavenger hunt. "Find it, post it, tag us" makes customers create the content. Do this more.

Fix

  • Gaps between posts. 15 posts and a "we're back" relaunch tell new visitors the account isn't active. Posting gaps also reset reach.
  • Bio doesn't prompt action. It has no show date, no "where to find us", no link, and no way to sell to you. It also mentions MTG, but every post is Pokémon.
  • Name collision. "Poke Stock" is close to PokeStocked, Poke Stocks, and the restock-alert pages. Searchers expect restock alerts, not a vendor.
  • Static announcement graphics. These got 1 like. Reels from the booth got about 18×.
  • Giveaways that farm follows. "Follow + tag friends" brings followers who don't buy, and they pull engagement down.
  • No inventory or buy list. Followers can't see what you have or what you pay for cards.

Strategy

Make each show weekend your content

Every month you spend two days at a table with hundreds of collectors walking by. That's where your content comes from. Shoot there, post through the month, and give people a reason to follow from the booth.

Cadence

  • 4 Reels per week, with all footage shot on show weekends. One show weekend gives you 20–30 clips.
  • 1 inventory carousel per week, every Tuesday, so followers learn when to check.
  • Stories daily: polls, price checks, "new in," countdown stickers before shows.
  • Cross-post Reels to TikTok and Facebook. Post show and inventory updates in the Arizona Pokemon TCG Facebook group (about 1.2K members).

Giveaways

Giveaways that bring buyers, not just followers

  • One per show, announced T−7 and drawn on show Sunday. Winners must pick up at the booth. This filters for local people and brings traffic.
  • Entry: share to Story + comment your chase card. Drop "tag 3 friends," which brings in tagged bots and people from out of state.
  • Prize: one sealed product people want, plus a "booth credit" second prize. Credit gets people to spend at the table.
  • Keep the scavenger hunt as the in-person format and repost every entry.
  • Measure it: followers gained, and how many are still following and engaging 14 days later. If retention drops below 60%, scale it back.
Partners

Collabs and creator seeding

  • Show organizer (@cactuscardsllc): ask for a vendor spotlight and use Collab posts for every show announcement. Their reels already reach the exact audience you need.
  • Co-vendors: trade shout-outs and do a monthly "vendor trade-off" reel.
  • Family and micro rip creators (Beforeskoolripz, lunch-break rippers, "1 pack a day until 1K" accounts): keep seeding 2–4 packs a month. In return, ask them to tag @poke.stock.az on Instagram (not just "Poke Stock Az" on Facebook) and to mention the next show.
  • Local shops that don't vend at shows: trade overflow and refer buy offers to each other.
